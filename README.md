# Royal English B1

**The British English Method**

**Author: prof Rodrigo Bastos Ferreira**

## Open the course

**[Launch Royal English B1](https://logica3000-pianist.github.io/royal-english-b1/)**

**[Download the complete 635-page book](https://logica3000-pianist.github.io/royal-english-b1/Royal_English_B1.pdf)**

Ten complete units with 40 lesson pages per unit, entirely in English.

- Part A: original reading articles, British listening and exercises.
- Part B: vocabulary, grammar, general English and business English practice.
- Part C: writing, tips, models and exercises.

400 lesson pages, 1,273 activities, 30 British recordings with subtitles, 20 contextual photographs across 113 lesson image placements, complete answer keys, browser progress/writing storage, full-course search and backup export/import.

The book includes 400 lesson pages plus front matter, listening scripts, credits and the final answer key. Listening links in the book open the matching public online lesson.

## Complete source and assets

The course is preserved in two complete, versioned packages:

- `royal-english-core.zip`: HTML, JavaScript, CSS, all ten course units, photographs, fonts, credits and the complete PDF.
- `royal-english-listening.zip`: all 30 MP3 recordings and synchronised subtitle files.

Extract both packages into the same directory to obtain the complete static application. The GitHub Pages workflow extracts and publishes all files automatically; learners receive ordinary fast-loading static pages and audio files.

Audio uses synthetic British English narration. Writing models are suggested responses, rather than automatic assessments. Image and font credits are included. Learner work stays in the browser unless the learner exports a backup.
